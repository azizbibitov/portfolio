Ashgabat 3D Map

A native iOS 3D map of Ashgabat built for a client: search, building and place cards, public transport, turn-by-turn driving navigation, day/night and weather, 3D building models and bridges, and an offline city package. Everything on the map comes from the backend and is rendered natively with MapLibre Native and Metal.

Features

What it does

The app has no map data of its own: tiles, style, colours, buildings, models, search, routes, light and weather all come from the backend, and the app renders them natively.

🏙️

3D City

.glb building models with two levels of detail, facade windows drawn by a shader, street sections with lamps that light the street at night, smooth 3D bridges and a sky layer.

🗺️

Server-Driven Map

The backend's MapLibre style in Russian or Turkmen, lane-level roads and markings, address labels from zoom 16, and a 2D/3D tilt.

🌗

Day, Night & Weather

Sun position, map theme, model lighting, haze, rain and snow, all computed by the server and applied to the map and the 3D models.

🔎

Search & Cards

Organizations, addresses, streets and categories, building cards with the organizations inside, bus stops and public transport routes.

🧭

Turn-by-Turn Navigation

Routes for car, foot and bicycle; voice guidance for driving with lane hints, speed limits and speed cameras, built on Ferrostar.

📦

Offline City

The backend's Ashgabat package (map and 3D models) downloads in the background and is used when there is no network.

Technology

Tech Stack

SwiftUI over MapLibre Native, with custom Metal layers for 3D models, bridges and sky, Ferrostar for routing and guidance, and host-run Swift test packages that compile the app's own sources.
