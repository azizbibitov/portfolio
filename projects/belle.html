iOS App

Belle

The iOS client for Belle, a C2C classifieds marketplace for Turkmenistan. Browse and search listings, find items by photo, post ads through a per-category sell flow, and promote them with in-app coins. Native SwiftUI on iOS 17+, with Liquid Glass on iOS 26.

Features

What it does

Guests browse and search freely; posting, favorites, contacting sellers and promotion sit behind a phone + OTP login.

🏠

Feed & Search

Category tiles and curated sections on the home feed, search with suggestions, filters driven by each category's fields, price and location, plus saved searches and viewing history.

📷

Photo Search

A camera "lens": point it at an item or pick a photo from the library, and the app shows similar listings from the marketplace.

📝

Dynamic Sell Flow

Posting an ad walks through category, server-defined fields for that category, photos from camera or gallery, a location picked on the map, and a preview before publishing.

🚀

Promotion & Coins

Sellers boost ads with bump and highlight services and visual card effects, paid with in-app BelleCoins. Balance, top-ups and a full transaction history live in the profile.

📊

Ad Analytics

Per-ad statistics: traffic sources, the hours buyers are active, the regions they view from, how the price compares to the category, and a sale forecast.

🔐

Link Login & Profile

Phone + OTP sign-in through the local Link messenger, favorites with private notes, a contacts journal, notifications, my-ads management, light and dark themes.

Technology

Tech Stack

SwiftUI with Swift 6 strict concurrency, MVVM + Coordinator navigation, and a typed REST layer on Alamofire with dependency injection through FactoryKit.
