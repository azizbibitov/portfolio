Features

What it does

One account covers films, series, TV channels and music, on the phone and on Apple TV, with up to five signed-in devices.

🎬

Films, Series & Live TV

A catalog of films, series, cartoons, anime, documentaries, karaoke, news and education with genre and year filters, plus live TV channels grouped by category with bookmarks.

▶️

Video Player

AVPlayer for films and series, MobileVLCKit for live TV channels whose MP2 audio AVFoundation can't decode, plus VAST ads, picture-in-picture and AirPlay.

🎵

Music Streaming

Playlists, artists and albums, a full-screen player with a queue, saved music, and a "can't find it?" request that sends a suggestion to the content team.

⬇️

Offline Downloads

Films and episodes download through AVAssetDownloadTask and are tracked in CoreData, with the storage they take shown in the library.

📺

Phone ↔ TV

Sign a TV in by entering its device code, manage the signed-in devices, and use the phone as a companion: search on the phone and start playback on the TV over a WebSocket.

👪

Profiles & Billing

Several profiles per account, including child profiles, an account balance with top-ups, auto-renewing subscriptions, transaction history and push notifications.

Technology

Tech Stack

SwiftUI with MVVM and Combine, a Moya networking layer, Factory for dependency injection, and a shared codebase for the iOS and tvOS apps.
